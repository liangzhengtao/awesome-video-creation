# Voiceover Production Skill

> Record and produce professional voiceovers that sound polished and engaging.

## When to Use

- Recording narration for tutorials and explainer videos
- Creating voiceovers for B-roll heavy content
- Producing podcast episodes or audio content
- Adding voice to animated or motion graphics videos
- Localizing content with voice dubbing
- Creating voiceovers for AI-generated video content

## Workflow

### Step 1: Voice Preparation

```markdown
Prepare your voice for recording:

WARM-UP ROUTINE (5 minutes):
1. Hum for 30 seconds (activate vocal cords)
2. Lip trills up and down scales (30 seconds)
3. Tongue twisters: "Red leather, yellow leather" x5
4. "La la la" scales (high to low, low to high)
5. Yawn and stretch jaw (relax tension)
6. Deep breathing: 4 count in, 7 hold, 8 out x3

HYDRATION:
- Drink room temperature water (not cold)
- Avoid dairy 2 hours before (causes mucus)
- Avoid caffeine right before (dries vocal cords)
- Keep water nearby during recording
- Apple or green tea helps with clarity

VOICE SETTINGS:
- Posture: Stand or sit upright
- Distance from mic: 6-8 inches (fist-width)
- Angle: Slightly off-axis (not directly into mic)
- Energy level: 20% more than conversation
```

### Step 2: Recording Setup

```markdown
Set up for voiceover recording:

MICROPHONE CHOICE:
- USB (budget): Blue Yeti, Audio-Technica AT2020USB+
- XLR (pro): Shure SM7B, Rode PodMic, Electro-Voice RE20
- Lavalier: Rode Wireless Go, DJI Mic

ROOM TREATMENT (Budget):
- Record in a closet full of clothes (free)
- Hang blankets on walls ($0-20)
- Use a reflection filter behind mic ($30-50)
- Place mic near soft furnishings, away from walls
- Close windows and doors
- Turn off HVAC/fans during recording

RECORDING SETTINGS:
- Sample Rate: 48kHz (video) or 44.1kHz (audio-only)
- Bit Depth: 24-bit
- Format: WAV (uncompressed)
- Gain: Peak levels at -12dB to -6dB
- Noise Floor: Below -60dB
```

### Step 3: Recording Technique

```markdown
Voiceover recording techniques:

MICROPHONE TECHNIQUE:
- Maintain consistent distance (use a pop filter as reference)
- Slightly off-axis to reduce plosives (P, B, T sounds)
- Lean in for emphasis, pull back for transitions
- Use a shock mount to reduce desk vibrations

DELIVERY TECHNIQUES:
- Smile while speaking (audible in tone)
- Gesture with hands (adds natural energy)
- Look at a point near the mic (not at screen)
- Read ahead - your eyes should be 1-2 lines ahead of your voice

PACING:
- Slower than conversational speed (but not slow)
- Pause at punctuation (commas = short, periods = medium)
- Emphasize key words (not every word)
- Vary speed - faster for exciting, slower for important

HANDLING MISTAKES:
- Pause, then repeat the sentence from the beginning
- Don't stop the recording - edit later
- Clap or snap to mark mistakes (visible in waveform)
```

### Step 4: Post-Production

```markdown
Process voiceover audio:

CHAIN ORDER (important):
1. **Noise Reduction**
   - Capture noise profile from silence
   - Apply gentle reduction (6-12dB)
   - Don't over-process (creates artifacts)

2. **EQ (Equalization)**
   - High-pass filter at 80Hz (remove rumble)
   - Cut 200-400Hz if muddy (-2 to -4dB)
   - Boost 2-5kHz for clarity (+2 to +3dB)
   - Boost 8-12kHz for air/brightness (+1 to +2dB)

3. **Compression**
   - Ratio: 3:1 to 4:1
   - Threshold: -18dB to -12dB
   - Attack: 5-10ms
   - Release: 50-100ms
   - Makeup gain to restore level

4. **De-essing**
   - Target frequency: 5-8kHz
   - Threshold: Just catches harsh S sounds
   - Reduction: 3-6dB

5. **Loudness Normalization**
   - Target: -16 LUFS (YouTube)
   - Target: -14 LUFS (Spotify)
   - True peak: -1dB maximum
```

### Step 5: Quality Control

```markdown
Voiceover quality checklist:

AUDIO QUALITY:
- [ ] No background noise audible
- [ ] No room echo or reverb
- [ ] No plosives (P/B pops)
- [ ] No sibilance (harsh S sounds)
- [ ] No mouth clicks or lip smacks
- [ ] Consistent volume throughout
- [ ] Clean edits (no audible cuts)

DELIVERY:
- [ ] Natural and conversational
- [ ] Appropriate energy and pacing
- [ ] Key words emphasized
- [ ] No mispronunciations
- [ ] Consistent tone throughout
- [ ] Emotion matches content

TECHNICAL:
- [ ] -16 LUFS loudness
- [ ] -1dB true peak maximum
- [ ] 48kHz sample rate
- [ ] 24-bit depth
- [ ] No clipping anywhere
```

## Pitfalls

### Common Mistakes

1. **Recording in an echoey room** - Bare walls create reverb; treat your space
2. **Inconsistent mic distance** - Moving closer/farther changes tone dramatically
3. **Too much processing** - Over-compressed voice sounds unnatural
4. **Not enough practice** - Record test takes before the real thing
5. **Ignoring plosives** - Pop filters are essential, not optional
6. **Editing too tight** - Removing all breaths sounds robotic
7. **Wrong loudness** - Too loud gets compressed by platforms; too quiet gets lost

### Quality Checks

- [ ] Voice sounds natural, not processed
- [ ] No distracting background sounds
- [ ] Consistent level from start to finish
- [ ] Words are clear and intelligible
- [ ] Energy matches the content
- [ ] Meets platform loudness standards

## Pro Tips

1. **Stand when recording** - Standing opens diaphragm and adds energy
2. **Record a "room tone"** - 10 seconds of silence for noise reduction
3. **Smile audibly** - A smile changes your voice tone positively
4. **Use a pop filter** - Always, no exceptions
5. **Batch record** - Record all voiceovers in one session for consistency
6. **The "water trick"** - Drink water between takes, not during
7. **Listen on earbuds** - Most viewers use earbuds; check your mix on them

1. **声音准备** - 热身、补水、设置

- 忽视爆破音
- 编辑过于紧凑

- 站立录制
- 录制"房间噪声"用于降噪
- 微笑时声音会改变
- 使用防喷罩
- 批量录制保持一致性
