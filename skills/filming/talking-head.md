# Talking Head Skill

> Film professional talking head videos that look and sound great.

## When to Use

- Recording YouTube videos with direct-to-camera presentation
- Creating educational or commentary content
- Filming course introductions and conclusions
- Recording interview-style content
- Making announcement or update videos
- Building personal brand through face-to-camera content

## Workflow

### Step 1: Setup Your Recording Space

```markdown
Optimize my recording setup:

SPACE: [room type and size]
BUDGET: [low / medium / high]
EXISTING GEAR: [camera, microphone, lights you own]

Evaluate and recommend:
1. Camera positioning and angle
2. Lighting setup (key, fill, back)
3. Background design
4. Acoustic treatment
5. Recording settings (resolution, frame rate, codec)
```

### Step 2: Camera and Framing

```markdown
Camera setup guide:

FRAMING RULES:
- Eye line: Top third of frame
- Headroom: Fist-width gap above head
- Look room: Space in direction of gaze
- Rule of thirds: Eyes on upper third line
- Background: 3-6 feet behind subject for depth

CAMERA SETTINGS:
- Resolution: 4K (downscale to 1080p in post for flexibility)
- Frame rate: 24fps (cinematic) or 30fps (standard)
- Aperture: f/2.8-f/4 (blur background, stay in focus)
- White balance: Match your lights (usually 5000-5600K)
- Shutter speed: 1/[frame rate x 2] (1/50 for 24fps)
```

### Step 3: Lighting Design

```markdown
Three-point lighting setup:

KEY LIGHT:
- Position: 45° to one side, slightly above eye level
- Diffusion: Softbox or diffusion panel
- Intensity: Main source, properly exposed

FILL LIGHT:
- Position: Opposite side of key, lower
- Intensity: 50-75% of key light (ratio determines mood)
- Alternative: Reflector panel for budget setup

BACK LIGHT / HAIR LIGHT:
- Position: Behind and above, opposite key light
- Purpose: Separate subject from background
- Intensity: Subtle, just enough for edge definition

BUDGET ALTERNATIVES:
- Key: Window + white curtain (free, best quality)
- Fill: White foam board reflector ($5)
- Back: LED strip or desk lamp with diffusion ($15)
```

### Step 4: On-Camera Performance

```markdown
Improve my on-camera delivery:

PREPARATION:
- Memorize key beats, not word-for-word script
- Practice the first 30 seconds until natural
- Have water nearby (room temperature)
- Warm up voice and face muscles

DELIVERY TECHNIQUES:
- Look at the lens, not the screen
- Speak slightly louder and more energetic than normal
- Use hand gestures (keep them in frame)
- Vary your pace - speed up for exciting parts, slow for emphasis
- Smile with your eyes (squinch technique)
- Nod when making key points

ENERGY MANAGEMENT:
- Take breaks between 5-10 minute segments
- Stand up if possible (more energy)
- Do a quick exercise break between takes
- Record your "A material" first when energy is highest
```

### Step 5: Recording Checklist

```markdown
Pre-recording checklist:

TECHNICAL:
- [ ] Camera battery charged (or plugged in)
- [ ] Memory card has space (minimum 30 minutes)
- [ ] Audio recording started (separate recorder or camera)
- [ ] Test recording played back (check audio and video)
- [ ] Focus locked on eyes (manual focus, not auto)
- [ ] White balance set and locked
- [ ] Room temperature comfortable (prevent sweating)

PRESENTATION:
- [ ] Script/notes visible (behind camera or teleprompter)
- [ ] Wardrobe checked (no busy patterns, solid colors work best)
- [ ] Hair and appearance checked
- [ ] Background checked (no distractions)
- [ ] Phone on silent, notifications off
- [ ] "Quiet - Recording" sign on door
```

## Pitfalls

### Common Mistakes

1. **Dead eyes** - Reading from script makes eyes dart; memorize beats instead
2. **Monotone delivery** - Vary pitch, pace, and energy throughout
3. **Bad framing** - Too much headroom, subject off-center
4. **Poor audio** - Echo-y room, too far from mic, background noise
5. **Distracting background** - Messy shelves, bright windows behind you
6. **Flat lighting** - Overhead room lights create unflattering shadows
7. **No energy** - Camera eats energy; you need 20% more than conversation

### Quality Checks

- [ ] Audio sounds clean with no echo or background noise
- [ ] Eyes are in the upper third of the frame
- [ ] Lighting is soft and flattering (no harsh shadows)
- [ ] Background is intentional and non-distracting
- [ ] Delivery feels natural, not robotic
- [ ] Camera is at eye level (not looking up or down)

## Pro Tips

1. **Shoot in 4K, deliver in 1080p** - Gives you room to reframe in post
2. **Record 10 seconds of silence** - Useful for noise reduction in editing
3. **Wear solid colors** - Patterns strobe on camera; solids look professional
4. **Use a catch light** - Small light reflection in eyes makes you look alive
5. **Stand vs. sit** - Standing gives more energy and natural gestures
6. **Mirror behind camera** - Place a face near the lens for natural eye contact
7. **Batch record** - Film multiple videos in one session while setup is ready

## 中文版本

### 何时使用

- 录制面对镜头的YouTube视频
- 创建教育或评论内容
- 录制课程介绍和总结
- 录制访谈风格内容
- 制作公告或更新视频
- 通过面对镜头的内容建立个人品牌

### 工作流程概要

1. **搭建录制空间** - 相机定位、灯光、背景、声学处理
2. **相机和构图** - 视线在上三分之一、头部留白、视线方向留空间
3. **灯光设计** - 三点布光：主光、补光、背光
4. **镜头前表现** - 看镜头、语速变化、手势、眼神交流
5. **录制检查清单** - 技术检查和表现准备

### 常见陷阱

- 眼神呆滞（死盯提词器）
- 语调单调
- 构图不佳
- 音频质量差
- 背景杂乱

### 专业建议

- 4K录制，1080p交付（后期可重新构图）
- 录制10秒静音用于降噪
- 穿纯色衣服
- 使用眼神光
- 站立录制以获得更多能量
- 批量录制多个视频
