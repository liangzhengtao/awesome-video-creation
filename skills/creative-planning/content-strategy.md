# Content Strategy Skill

> Build a sustainable video content strategy that grows your audience.

## When to Use

- Starting a new YouTube channel or video series
- Planning quarterly or monthly content calendars
- Analyzing what content performs best in your niche
- Developing a consistent publishing rhythm
- Repurposing content across multiple platforms
- Building a content moat that competitors can't easily replicate

## Workflow

### Step 1: Niche and Audience Analysis

```markdown
Help me define my video content niche and audience:

MY EXPERTISE: [your skills/knowledge areas]
MY INTERESTS: [what you enjoy creating]
MY GOALS: [monetization / brand building / education / community]

Analyze:
1. Where these three overlap (ideal niche)
2. Target audience persona (age, problems, aspirations)
3. Content gap opportunities in this niche
4. 5 channels doing this well (study their strategy)
5. Unique angle I could take

Output: A one-paragraph niche definition and audience persona.
```

### Step 2: Content Pillar Development

```markdown
Based on my niche: [niche definition]

Create 4-6 content pillars (categories) for my channel:

For each pillar:
1. Pillar name
2. Purpose (attract / nurture / convert / retain)
3. Content types within this pillar
4. Example video titles (5 per pillar)
5. SEO keyword clusters
6. Posting frequency recommendation

Format as a content pillar framework I can reference monthly.
```

### Step 3: Content Calendar Generation

```markdown
Generate a [monthly/quarterly] content calendar:

CHANNEL: [channel name and niche]
UPLOAD SCHEDULE: [2x/week / 3x/week / daily]
CONTENT PILLARS: [list your pillars]

For each planned video:
1. Title (SEO-optimized, curiosity-driven)
2. Content pillar it belongs to
3. Target keyword
4. Estimated search volume potential
5. Content type (tutorial / list / story / review)
6. Thumbnail concept (2-3 words)
7. Production complexity (simple / medium / complex)
8. Best publishing day/time

Balance: 60% searchable content, 40% trending/cultural content
```

### Step 4: Repurposing Strategy

```markdown
Design a content repurposing plan:

PRIMARY CONTENT: [YouTube video / Podcast / Blog post]

Transform into:
1. YouTube Shorts (3 clips minimum)
2. TikTok format
3. Instagram Reels
4. LinkedIn post/video
5. Twitter/X thread
6. Blog post / Newsletter
7. Pinterest pins
8. Community post

For each format, specify:
- What to extract from original
- Format adjustments needed
- Optimal length/dimensions
- Platform-specific hooks
- Posting timing relative to original
```

### Step 5: Growth Analysis Framework

```markdown
Create a video performance analysis framework:

Metrics to track weekly:
1. Views (new vs. returning audience)
2. Click-through rate (CTR)
3. Average view duration (AVD)
4. Audience retention curve patterns
5. Subscriber conversion rate
6. Revenue per mille (RPM)

Analysis prompts:
- What patterns do top 10% videos share?
- Which content pillar performs best?
- What's the optimal video length for my niche?
- When do viewers drop off most?
- Which thumbnails get highest CTR?

Output: A simple spreadsheet template structure for tracking.
```

## Pitfalls

### Common Mistakes

1. **Chasing virality over consistency** - Viral spikes don't build sustainable channels
2. **Too many content pillars** - 4-6 is ideal; more dilutes your brand
3. **Ignoring search intent** - Creating what you want, not what viewers search for
4. **No content moat** - Content anyone could make with zero expertise
5. **Inconsistent publishing** - Algorithms and audiences reward consistency
6. **Platform-agnostic strategy** - Each platform needs format-specific optimization
7. **Analysis paralysis** - Over-planning instead of shipping and iterating

### Quality Checks

- [ ] Does each video serve a clear content pillar?
- [ ] Is there a balance between searchable and trending content?
- [ ] Can you realistically produce this volume with quality?
- [ ] Does the strategy differentiate from competitors?
- [ ] Are there clear success metrics defined?

## Pro Tips

1. **The 70/20/10 rule** - 70% proven formats, 20% adjacent experiments, 10% wild bets
2. **Series create habits** - Recurring series (e.g., "Weekly Tips") train viewers to return
3. **Steal audience, not ideas** - Find where your audience already gathers; create for them
4. **Title first, content second** - If you can't write a compelling title, the video won't work
5. **Batch by pillar** - Film all Pillar 1 videos in one session, Pillar 2 in another
6. **Study your outliers** - Your best and worst performers teach more than averages
7. **Content stacking** - Each video should reference 2-3 others, creating a content web

## 中文版本

### 何时使用

- 开始新的YouTube频道或视频系列
- 规划季度或月度内容日历
- 分析细分领域表现最佳的内容
- 建立一致的发布节奏
- 跨平台内容再利用
- 构建竞争对手难以复制的内容护城河

### 工作流程概要

1. **细分市场与受众分析** - 定义理想细分领域和受众画像
2. **内容支柱开发** - 建立4-6个内容类别框架
3. **内容日历生成** - 规划每月/每季度的发布计划
4. **再利用策略** - 将主要内容转化为多种格式
5. **增长分析框架** - 建立性能追踪和分析体系

### 常见陷阱

- 追求病毒式传播而非持续性
- 内容支柱过多导致品牌稀释
- 忽视搜索意图
- 发布频率不稳定

### 专业建议

- 70/20/10法则：70%验证有效，20%相关实验，10%大胆尝试
- 系列内容培养观众习惯
- 先写标题，再做内容
- 按支柱批量制作内容
